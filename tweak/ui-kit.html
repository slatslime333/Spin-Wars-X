Locked

1. Stadium stays The bowl in app.js renderNewBattle (#newBattleSvg, viewBox 0 0 100 100) is the playfield. Physics uses those polygons. We overlay CSS on #newStadium. We do not redraw pockets, X-Rail, or the bowl in AI art.
2. Pocket orientation is the live one X-Rail / X-Exit at the top. Left Over, center Xtreme, right Over at the bottom. Mockups that rotate the stadium or put holes on the sides are wrong. Do not match those.
3. GitHub-possible Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, Kanit + Staatliches, existing blade/bit/ratchet PNGs. Cream windows, thick ink outlines, green stamps. No 3D, no new illustrated stadium, no extra asset pipeline.
4. PC and every phone One token kit, two layouts. Desktop: overlays on a large stadium, launch as a side pane. Phone: existing locked 100dvh shell, leftover stadium square, Dash/ability in the thumb zone. Do not ship iPad mockup proportions.
5. One kit for every screen Battle overlay is the source of truth. Home, league, drafts, VS, launch, Rogue hub/shop/scenario, summaries, help, and DEV all reuse the same window / stamp / list / bar / dialogue pieces.

5. How we ship it

  1. Approve this kit. If a token, overlay, or piece is wrong, change it here first. Do not start from a new mockup stadium.
  2. Move tokens into style.css once approved. Keep class names (.swx-window, .swx-stamp, .swx-row, .swx-dialogue, .swx-nameplate, .swx-bar).
  3. Family 1 — battle + launch. Overlay nameplates on .stadium-slot. Shrink the three HUD cards. Launch: desktop side pane, phone bottom sheet. Physics and SVG untouched.
  4. Family 2 — VS + combo check. Bigger sprites, overlapping tickets, LIVE strip, LET IT RIP stamp. Same OVR/META/HIT boxes, less lecture.
  5. Family 3 — picks. Hero + list. Blade / ratchet / bit / Rogue tier all use .swx-row.
  6. Family 4 — home + league. Stadium as wallpaper (the live SVG or a CSS crop of it), small corner menu of stamps. Campaign stays locked.
  7. Family 5 — Rogue extras. Shop, hub, scenario, summaries, omen, help, DEV. Same windows. Do not invent a second look.

Prove each family in the browser on a wide desktop and a phone-width viewport before starting the next. Dash/ability stay in the thumb zone. Ability charges stay 2 per match.
