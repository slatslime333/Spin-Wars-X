# Spin Wars X

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS Beyblade X–style stadium game. There is no package manager or build step.

## Cursor Cloud specific instructions

- Serve the repo root over HTTP (`python3 -m http.server 8080`) and open `index.html`. Scripts are cache-busted (`style.css?v=9.72`, `presentation.css?v=9.72`, `fx.css?v=9.70`, `xrail-engine.js?v=9.25`, `app.js?v=9.71`, `movement-engine.js?v=9.35`, `vs-commentary.js?v=9.52`, `abilities.js?v=9.71`, `scoreboard.js?v=9.27`, `rogue-mode.js?v=9.71`); hard-refresh after pulling. A service worker (`sw.js`) plus `manifest.webmanifest` make it a Safari home-screen app: Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it once online so the worker caches the game, fonts, and art; after that it plays offline. Needs HTTPS (or localhost). Battle on a phone is a locked `100dvh` shell (no page scroll). Dash/ability sit in the thumb zone. Health bars keep ratchet · bit as text (no part art). Tapping a YOU/CPU HUD card opens an inspect drop with stats, Rogue modifiers/toys, and live ability or situation timers. A thin momentum bar under each RPM bar fills from live speed/velocity. RPM bars keep a faint amber trail that falls slower than the live fill on RPM loss, and a cyan chunk that lands ahead of the fill on RPM gain (Hurricane). The stadium square fills leftover space between the LIVE booth and the HUD, including launch setup. Aero Pegasus aim is a bottom-right overlay stick that unmounts when the blast fires; the hit marker is a glowy circle (no crosshair), ~30% smaller than the doubled ring. A graze of that marker against the foe disc is a hit. Lift (vanish) is 1.3s, then 3s to aim. CPU Pegasus does not spawn the marker at a random far point or stick-home every blast: it starts on a miss offset (~0.32–0.60) from the live player, follows that offset through lift/aim, and hits some grazes without a lock-on. Hurricane radius is `STORM_R=0.714` (15% smaller than 0.84). Double Edge +KB also deals 1–4 extra RPM (skip if the victim ignores clash RPM). Iron Skin is 2.5s. Free Spin is 15% per clash. Ability charges are 2 per match and stay spent only for that match (mode + blades); a new Quick Play or league match starts at 2/2.
- Blade art is optional. If `BLADE_ENGINE` has a `sprite` path, combo cards and the battle model use that PNG; otherwise the gold/silver circle stays. Phoenix Wing stays on `assets/blades/phienix_wing1.png`. Other blades map to the PNGs in `assets/blades/` (Leon Claw uses `Leonfang.png`, Shelter Drake uses `shelterdrake.png` (cropped to fill the sprite box; the original file had extra padding so the battle disc looked small), Dran Sword uses `Dransword (1).png`, Shark Scale uses `sharkscale.png` on the hidden Rogue boss kit). Battle sprites are sized to the circle (`r=4.85`) and spin clockwise from live RPM until 0. A blade may set `battleSpriteScale` for visual-only size (hit size stays `r=4.85`). Live battle Beys draw a faint, short (~220ms) gold/slate motion trail under the model so the path is readable on the light bowl; it is visual-only and does not change physics.
- Bit art lives in `assets/blades/png bit/`. Selectable bits with a PNG (Flat, Low Flat, Rush, Point, Hexa, Level, Wedge, Kick, Ball, Orb, Needle, Quake) show on bit pick cards and the VS combo plates. Low Rush still has no dedicated file. Taper / High Needle / Elevate are not selectable.
- Ratchet art lives in `assets/blades/ratchets/` as `{number}-{height}.png`. 60-height photos are the source; 70 and 80 keep the same shape with a different color combo per sides×height. Pick cards and VS plates use the matching height file.
- Blade pick cards tint by `tier` (gold / silver / bronze). The blade sits in its own art slot (`max-width`/`max-height` 100%, `object-fit: contain`) so the full circular Bey stays in view — do not stretch `width:100%` or Safari clips the bottom of the photo. Draft is an inspect platter plus a scrolling roster of rows (disc, name, type, stat ticks, OVR). Full stats, ability, and META live on the inspect panel. Home is a night-stadium lobby: bowl and title fill the arena pane, then a 2×2 gate (Rogue, Quick Play, locked Campaign, How to Play). Quick Play is a league board (featured Quick Match, then Bronze / Silver / Gold / Custom plates). Rogue landing is a locker: New Game / Continue / Run Scoreboard / How a run works — not a third copy of the league list. Campaign is locked. Continue is greyed until a run is saved in `localStorage` plus a `swx_rogue` cookie. Run Scoreboard stays on the landing even with no finished runs; it opens the archive list (`spinWarsX.rogue.runs.v1`, won or lost — not RUNNING). Closing the browser resumes that screen; a mid-battle close restarts the battle with the saved score. Ability charges are 2 per match and stay spent if you leave and come back to that same match (session + Rogue save, keyed by mode and blades). A new Quick Play or league match always starts at 2/2; they do not refill mid-match. Coming back to a live battle kicks the frame loop and finishes a leftover sword/Pegasus hold so the Beys do not freeze. Eighteen matches, each first to 7, then endless after Shark Scale (hidden Gold, Rogue final boss only — always 1-60 Ball). Beat match 18 and CLAIM SHARK SCALE or KEEP your Bey; the night does not end. Minis at 6 and 12. New Game opens Bronze / Silver / Gold (one line of curve each), then three random blades from that tier, three random ratchets, three random bits — same draft as Quick Play. Every starter opens in Bronze form. Silver and Gold keep their shape (`makeStartScale` only pulls highs toward Bronze); they evolve on a window (Silver after match 3–4, hard at 6, then Enhance ~8–12; Gold Bronze→Silver after 3–4, hard at 6, then Gold form ~9–12, hard at 12). Bronze cannot evolve — Enhance after match 5, hard pity at 6 and 12 (+5 best 3 / +3 rest). Gold never Enhances. Form cards stop in endless (`matchIndex > 18`). Dry-hub pity only counts while a form card is actually available. Rogue pick cards show luck A/B/C. Shop commons are sacrifices (+2/−1 or burst). Uncommons are tradeoffs/consumables. Zombie is 3 games, one respawn per point on a spin finish (Over/Xtreme still kill). Player rares can be a clean bump (`plus3` / `plus1plus1`) plus reforge / ability swap / Hells Chain / Comeback / dash CD / extra charge — rare is rare for a reason. Gold shop stays thin because evolve is the paycheck. Combo VS plates are reused before every match. The CPU plate prints a pressure line from the night roll (“You've got this.” / “Even fight.” / “They're ahead.” / “A dark presence.” on the final). Rogue tints HIT/HOLD/MOVE only for upgrades (stat bumps, reforge, evolve, enhance, modifier) — not for CPU match-scale. One brain in `rogue-mode.js` owns CPU stacks and scale (`cpuNightRoll` / `cpuStackLead` / `fillCpuScale`). Overlay files must not rebuild stacks or rescale. Soft fill is 48% of the gap, cap ±7–8, identity bias. Pre-final nights ~0.94–1.07; match 18 band 1.08 for every starter. Early Gold is slightly easier (still can lose). Early Bronze is spicy, not a wall you bounce off at the same night. Bronze eases later as the shop snowballs; Gold squeezes later without becoming unbeatable. CPU hidden scale keeps the Bey's shape (Attack stays attacky). Rogue CPU parts can be messy on match 1; by match 3 they usually commit (Bronze match 3 is a coin flip, full at 4 — Attack + Rush/Flat on 60/70, Defense/Stamina + Ball/Needle/Hexa on 60 — not Knight Shield 5-80 Quake). After each hub pick, the next CPU takes a real card for each stat upgrade you locked in (rolled, not copied). Consumables, reforges, kit swaps, and skipped shops give a weaker +1 instead of a full card, then 0–3 extra from starter + match + a per-fight night. Night mix follows the starter: Bronze ~25% hard early and fewer hard nights later; Gold fewer hard nights early and a late squeeze. Minis and Shark keep spike nights. Bronze extra stays small and shrinks late (the player shop is the snowball). Gold extra grows later. Minis add stacks; match 18 extra is +3. Those tint and box. CPU power is often a bit above the player, sometimes on par, rarely a bit below — every starter tier stays a challenge. All starters sit in the same power neighborhood by Shark Scale. Picked stat upgrades also get their own stack boxes on the VS plate (net ATK +4 if you took +2 twice). An active modifier prints its name and short blurb on the plate. After a Rogue match win, before the shop opens, a 10% chance of a sideline scenario (tuner, parts dealer, rival talk, shortcut, coin flip, shop secret, double-or-nothing, autograph, clearance, slip, salvage, lube, stadium crew, coupon). Choices that change stats or the shop list the outcome (and % when it is a roll); flavor-only picks (autograph, rival) do not. DEV GAME CONDITIONS can force any of them. DEV SCENE SKIP MATCH 17 jumps to that match’s VS the same way FINAL BOSS does (stops a live battle, does not open a fake shop). Rare hub offers can be ability swap (~16% of rares): two random kits from the pool, BACK keeps the current one (does not consume the offer). A corner DEV button lists every upgrade/modifier for add/remove playtest. Quick Play opens the league list (Quick Match, Bronze / Silver / Gold / Custom). Quick Match rolls random player and CPU combos on the VS plates with reroll on both sides, plus PLAY and Back. Combo check is a LIVE commentary box above the VS plates, then LET IT RIP; VS copy is 2–3 short sentences (what the Beys are trying to do, first to 7) without stat-number lectures. Plates show boxed OVR and META plus boxed HIT/HOLD/MOVE stats. Battle chrome is the same LIVE booth as the VS plates, then stadium, then names with ratchet · bit name (text only), RPM with a META box on the health bar row, then launch controls in `#launchDock`. The booth holds a line for a beat instead of rewriting every frame: one event, one line (launch, clash, rail, recover, finish), witty not a lecture, with an occasional late-point prediction. Quality ROLL still has BACK to VS. After the quality reveal, angle/technique has LET IT RIP only — no BACK to reroll. The scoreboard is `0 VS 0` with `first to 7` underneath (Rogue shows match number there). After first-to-7, Quick Play and Rogue open a Match Summary (RPM damage, ability damage, big impacts, finishes, X-Rail rides, then a sequence multiplier). Dashes and X-Rail rides are listed at +0. Game quality at the end uses the launch-quality tiers (Horrible / Bad / Okay / Good / Perfect) from how the player did and points scored. Rogue landing always shows a Run Scoreboard door under Continue; it opens finished runs (`spinWarsX.rogue.runs.v1`, won or lost — not RUNNING) even when the list is empty. Tapping a run opens that run’s summary. Rogue run totals and boss bonuses live on a separate run summary when the run ends. Scoring constants are in `scoreboard.js` SCORE and `tweak/spin-wars-x-scoreboard.csv`. Physics are unchanged. Over finish overlay is flames; Xtreme is lightning; spin finish keeps the spinning ring.
- Idle RPM drain slightly speeds up as remaining spin falls so two tanks at ~20% RPM do not last as long as they did at full spin. Early-fight drain stays similar. Two non-Attack bits get an extra clash shove (`1.55`, still under cap) so they can occasionally Over/Xtreme from free movement with real momentum, without flying like Attack. Taper and High Needle are not selectable (Elevate already out).
- Drop launches hang **under the top X-Rail, beside the X-Exit** (not on the rail lip and not inside the V), then shoot straight toward stadium middle. Launch quality is that shot's accuracy (Perfect is tight, Horrible is wide). Stadium side still picks left vs right. Rail contact is skipped only while the drop is stalling.
- The seven combo stats set potential, not a flat tax. OVR and META stay display-only. Burst is unused in battle. Battle uses each stat against the opponent's matching number, not against 99: Attack answers Defense (RPM), Knockback answers Balance (shove), Stamina vs Stamina, Mobility vs Mobility. 70 vs 70 plays at the same intensity as 94 vs 94 (94 is a bit denser). A graze barely spends that gap; a real smash can spend most of it. 99 vs 60 should feel like a mismatch on a hit that counts, not a one-graze delete; a 500 vs 70 still hits a ceiling. Attack converts contact into RPM loss. Knockback converts contact into shove inside cap `0.086`. Defense soaks RPM loss. Balance soaks shove. Stamina slows idle spin drain. Mobility slightly raises cruise/launch response, not orbit width. Bit, launch, and physics still decide the round.
- Knockback is the existing clash bounce: incoming momentum plus Knockback. Cancel the approaching contact, then bounce that component back so a head-on clash does not zero both Beys. Keep each Bey's own orbit tangent so they bounce and settle instead of swapping spin direction or following the same line. Always un-overlap even during the hit lock so they cannot phase through. Hits should shove with real weight (cap `0.086`, still below the `0.128` stadium launch) without skating the bowl like air hockey. Attack-type bits shove non-attack bits harder (`1.24`) and take less tank recoil (`0.82`); two non-Attack bits get a `1.55` shove so tank vs tank can still pocket from a free-space hit without flying like Attack. A free X-Exit swing into a clash adds a light `1.11` shove (`1.20` if the swinging Bey is on an Attack bit). The swinging Bey then dumps leftover inbound speed so they bounce instead of riding through into a pocket; the victim shove and cap stay the same. While that swing is live (exit ramp or just after release), a non-Attack bit uses the same Attack clash role (`1.24` / `0.82` and the `1.20` spin term) so a Hexa/Ball coming off the ring hits like an Attack bit off the ring. Cap still `0.086`. Riding the rail is not that swing. Live Defense and Balance fade with remaining RPM so dying Beys take a little more RPM loss and knock instead of chip-stalling. `impactMomentumState` follows the shove that landed on that Bey (not its own outgoing knock) but must not freeze orbit off — ease back into the ring (soft bowl) even while they are still sliding. Speed-target damping must not eat impact velocity while `impactMomentumState` is high. Separating overlaps still must not stack.
- Xtreme/Over need a knock into the opening (`lastImpactForce` at least `0.020`), not a parked clip. Smash credit lasts about 1.1s and needs force `0.028`. A smash that strong can still score after hit-stun fades. Light force only counts while they are still in the shove. Crossing a painted hole is the attempt: clash tangent is allowed (do not require a perfect radial line). Mouth-force hits after an X-Exit/X-Rail swing count as a clash, not rail carry. Classify as smash when force is `0.028` and heading is at least `0.28` off the rail (`0.12` in free play); otherwise rail or dump. Recovery decides climb vs score — do not silently bounce a real hole entry. Occupying a painted hole (center in, or a knocked disc overlapping the rim) is a made pocket: Over/Xtreme or recovery, not a sit. Light/parked clips can still bounce. A knock into Over/Xtreme at 80%+ RPM recovers about `0.45` (straight smash a bit less, glance a bit more). Smash is still the easiest KO. Generic rail pockets and healthy limp dumps climb more than a smash. `inCommittedFinishMouth` skips the solid bounce so a mouth-force knock can finish crossing (outward `0.0012`). Backside rail still does not re-hook. An X-Exit swing that then clashes gets a slight knock boost (`1.11`, still capped at `0.086`). The swinging attacker dumps leftover follow-through after the bounce (no keepClashSpeed restore of the rail line; smash credit on themselves is capped below mouth force). Non-Attack bits on that swing use the Attack clash role so they hit with similar weight. Show RECOVERED over IMPACT/launch callouts when it happens. There is no 1.20s launch invuln bounce; X-Rail spawn sits on the rail, not in a hole. Do not add a timer to prevent self-KO.
- Live orbit follows Bit type, then stats. Home radius is a soft radial tendency, not a locked path. Free movement is a cartesian blend toward launch+RPM speed (`physicalSpeedTarget`) with a soft bowl toward home radius. Attack is slightly inside the rail at full RPM so they can still ride the X-Rail. By 80% RPM the ring is still wide enough to hook, but a bit inside and a bit slower than a fresh launch. By 50% RPM it sits under the X-Rail/X-Exit so laps miss the ring. By the last 20% RPM Attack pulls into a tight ring like Non-Attack, with a little leftover aggression. Tired Attack bits must actually walk in — leftover rail-width speed is bled when they are outside home. Orbit must yield to knockback the same way Defense/Stamina do. Non-Attack Center launches start a bit wide (under the X-Rail, never a rail sling) then walk in quickly; Ball/Orb stay shorter and tighter than Point/Level. By 30% RPM every Non-Attack bit sits on the center pin so two tired tanks meet and clash instead of circling past each other. After a smash they can be moved off that ring toward Over/Xtreme without skating like a hockey puck, then settle. Do not polar-rewrite heading into a perfect circle. X-Exit and free hits on the closer bounce toward center with most leftover speed and a slightly stronger shove to middle. While `railEngaged`, ride speed is owned by the X-Rail engine; do not cruise-damp it. Cruise accelerates up to `physicalSpeedTarget` and only bleeds excess; skip it while impact/exit is hot. A smash or X-Exit/rail knock that is actually flying at Over/Xtreme can trigger a short slo-mo zoom (~50%, once per point, not for spin or center pokes). A player dash has a 40% chance to reuse that same inner-lens cam for 1.5s (CPU dashes do not). The green stadium outline stays fixed; an inner lens zooms toward the impact and is clipped so it never covers the HUD. Impact during that cam gets a small shake. Physics ticks stay `1/60`; only the clock feeding them slows. Stadium CSS size still must not scale knock.
- X-Exit leaves the rail toward left-center, center, or right-center with a small heading change. Do not force every exit down the exact middle. Do not steer Beys off a pocket after X-Exit. If that line hits Over/Xtreme, roll: 35% self-KO at 80%+ RPM, 45% from 60–79%, 70% below 60%. A smash after they left the ring is still a smash. Do not add a timer to prevent self-KO. A free Bey that hits the X-Exit bounces toward stadium center with most of its speed and a slightly stronger shove to middle; it rides only if the existing hook already has a real CCW bite and momentum into the rail.
- Only the three painted holes (left Over, center Xtreme, right Over) can be left by a real outward smash. Wall, X-Rail mouth, and score share those SVG polygons. The rest of the lower X-Rail and wall stay solid. Light taps still should not score. A finish must actually cross into the hole, not graze the lower-rail rectangles that used to sit above the art.
- X-Rail capture uses remaining CCW + inward bite, not peak RPM. A real entry still has to hook from the inner face; the bite/speed bar is a little easier so near-misses ride instead of bouncing. Do not capture from the back of the rail or while already in a painted hole. Failed hooks bounce off the rail softer and keep a little CCW so a high-RPM Attack can still wind on instead of skating straight off. Low remaining RPM raises that hook bar so tired wide laps bounce instead of riding out the match. X-Exit closer still sends them toward middle with some orbit tangent. Ride speed is the speed they arrived with plus a light RPM drive. Exit speed is that carried rail speed. A rail-break ejector shoves toward stadium middle, not out through a pocket.
- Direct Clash aims at the opponent after both Beys spawn. Launch quality is aim accuracy (Perfect is tight, Horrible is wide). `impactMomentumState` stays high so they fly at each other instead of immediately orbiting.
- Center launches stay in the middle of the bowl but sit slightly toward that player's stadium side so two Center picks cannot spawn on top of each other. Attack-type bits still wind out toward their wide ring. Non-Attack bits wind into their own tighter orbit and must not sling out to the X-Rail like Attack. A small spin-correct tangent, not a random throw.
- X-Rail launches still start at the live lower corner via `SpinWarsXRailEngine.nearest`. Do not add a second invisible wall.
- CPU launch is locked at round start from the player's past techniques/tilts, never the live pick. Center-heavy habits get more X-Rail/Drop/Clash; X-Rail habits get rail contests and clashes, not Center; Clash habits get Center/rail/drop answers; Drop habits get Center and clash. CPU also avoids repeating itself. Dash and active kits follow that blade's personality (aggression/control/risk plus a kit plan): tanks hold dash, Attack/Flame dash into clashes, Pegasus and Iron Skin save charges, Hurricane waits until RPM is actually down. The last charge is usually clutched until the CPU is behind by 2 or the player is at 5. CPU also reads the player's dashes and ability pops this match when it picks launches and when it spends.
- Lint/test/build: there is no project linter or test runner. Prove changes with a browser battle (Drop, Direct Clash, and X-Rail) plus any one-off `node` geometry asserts against `xrail-engine.js`. Stat-gap checks can `node` the `SpinWarsStatMath` helpers (par 0.90, 99 vs 60 clearly above par, graze spend << smash).
