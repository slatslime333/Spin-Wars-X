# Spin Wars X

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS Beyblade X–style stadium game. There is no package manager or build step.

## Cursor Cloud specific instructions

- Serve the repo root over HTTP (`python3 -m http.server 8080`) and open `index.html`. Scripts are cache-busted (`style.css?v=8.86`, `xrail-engine.js?v=8.86`, `app.js?v=8.86`, `movement-engine.js?v=8.86`, `vs-commentary.js?v=8.86`, `rogue-mode.js?v=8.86`); hard-refresh after pulling.
- Blade art is optional. If `BLADE_ENGINE` has a `sprite` path, combo cards and the battle model use that PNG; otherwise the gold/silver circle stays. Phoenix Wing stays on `assets/blades/phienix_wing1.png`. Other blades map to the PNGs in `assets/blades/` (Leon Claw uses `Leonfang.png`, Shelter Drake uses `shelterdrake.png`, Dran Sword uses `Dransword (1).png`). Battle sprites are sized to the circle (`r=4.85`) and spin clockwise from live RPM until 0. Live battle Beys draw a faint, short (~220ms) gold/slate motion trail under the model so the path is readable on the light bowl; it is visual-only and does not change physics.
- Bit art lives in `assets/blades/png bit/`. Selectable bits with a PNG (Flat, Low Flat, Rush, Point, Hexa, Level, Wedge, Kick, Ball, Orb, Needle, Quake) show on bit pick cards and the VS combo plates. Low Rush still has no dedicated file. Taper / High Needle / Elevate are not selectable.
- Ratchet art lives in `assets/blades/ratchets/` as `{number}-60.png`. Pick cards and VS plates use that 60-height photo for 60, 70, and 80 until height recolors exist.
- Blade pick cards tint by `tier` (gold / silver / bronze). Home is a dark stadium lobby: SPIN WARS with a green X, line “Two Beys. X-Rail. First to 7.”, then LET IT RIP (Quick Play), locked Campaign, and Rogue. Quick Play is a league board (featured Quick Match, then Bronze / Silver / Gold / Custom plates). Rogue landing is New Game / Continue / a Help link — not a third copy of the league list. Campaign is locked. Continue is greyed until a run is saved in `localStorage` plus a `swx_rogue` cookie. Closing the browser resumes that screen; a mid-battle close restarts the battle with the saved score. Eighteen matches, each first to 7, then infinite after Shark Scale (hidden Gold, Rogue final boss only). Minis at 6 and 12. Starter is blade-only (random ratchet and bit). Silver and Gold open in Bronze form (stats pulled toward Bronze); they evolve, Bronze takes Enhanced instead. Rogue pick cards show luck A/B/C. Gold shop stays thin because evolve is the paycheck. Combo VS plates are reused before every match. Rogue tints HIT/HOLD/MOVE only for upgrades (stat bumps, reforge, evolve, enhance, modifier) — not for CPU match-scale. CPU hidden scale keeps the Bey's shape (Attack stays attacky). After each hub pick, the next CPU takes the same number of upgrades you have locked in (rolled, not copied), plus starting-tier pressure: Bronze 1–3 extras, Silver 1–2, Gold 1. Those tint and box. Bronze CPU power sits a tad higher; Silver a little harder; Gold stays as it was. Picked stat upgrades also get their own stack boxes on the VS plate (net ATK +4 if you took +2 twice). An active modifier prints its name and short blurb on the plate. A corner DEV button lists every upgrade/modifier for add/remove playtest. Quick Play opens the league list (Quick Match, Bronze / Silver / Gold / Custom). Quick Match rolls random player and CPU combos on the VS plates with reroll on both sides, plus PLAY and Back. Combo check is a LIVE commentary box above the VS plates, then LET IT RIP; the booth uses one of five intros, talks likes/dislikes on parts and stats, then picks a winner. Plates show boxed OVR and META plus boxed HIT/HOLD/MOVE stats. Battle chrome is the same LIVE booth as the VS plates, then stadium, then names with ratchet · bit name (text only), RPM with a META box on the health bar row, then launch controls in `#launchDock`. The booth holds a line for a beat instead of rewriting every frame: launch-pad copy talks about the series (first rip is a cold open; later rips react to the score), the first live seconds call the launches, then clashes/rail/recovery/finishes, with an occasional late-point prediction. Quality ROLL still has BACK to VS. After the quality reveal, angle/technique has LET IT RIP only — no BACK to reroll. The scoreboard is `0 VS 0` with `first to 7` underneath (Rogue shows match number there). Over finish overlay is flames; Xtreme is lightning; spin finish keeps the spinning ring.
- Idle RPM drain slightly speeds up as remaining spin falls so two tanks at ~20% RPM do not last as long as they did at full spin. Early-fight drain stays similar. Two non-Attack bits get an extra clash shove (`1.55`, still under cap) so they can occasionally Over/Xtreme from free movement with real momentum, without flying like Attack. Taper and High Needle are not selectable (Elevate already out).
- Drop launches hang **under the top X-Rail, beside the X-Exit** (not on the rail lip and not inside the V), then shoot straight toward stadium middle. Launch quality is that shot's accuracy (Perfect is tight, Horrible is wide). Stadium side still picks left vs right. Rail contact is skipped only while the drop is stalling.
- The seven combo stats refine existing battle terms; OVR and META stay display-only. Battle uses each stat against the opponent's matching number, not against 99: 70 vs 70 plays at the same intensity as 94 vs 94, and 99 knock vs 88 defense should feel like 60 knock vs 50 defense. The ratio is soft-capped so a 99 vs 75 is favored, not a bye. Attack converts contact into RPM loss. Knockback converts contact into shove inside cap `0.086`. Defense absorbs RPM loss and slightly resists knock. Stamina slows idle spin drain. Balance holds stability after hits. Mobility slightly raises cruise/launch response, not orbit width. Burst is unused in battle. Bit, launch, and physics still decide the round.
- Knockback is the existing clash bounce: incoming momentum plus Knockback/Attack stats. Cancel the approaching contact, then bounce that component back so a head-on clash does not zero both Beys. Keep each Bey's own orbit tangent so they bounce and settle instead of swapping spin direction or following the same line. Always un-overlap even during the hit lock so they cannot phase through. Hits should shove with real weight (cap `0.086`, still below the `0.128` stadium launch) without skating the bowl like air hockey. Attack-type bits shove non-attack bits harder (`1.24`) and take less tank recoil (`0.82`); two non-Attack bits get a `1.55` shove so tank vs tank can still pocket from a free-space hit without flying like Attack. A free X-Exit swing into a clash adds a light `1.11` shove. While that swing is live (exit ramp or just after release), a non-Attack bit uses the same Attack clash role (`1.24` / `0.82` and the `1.20` spin term) so a Hexa/Ball coming off the ring hits like an Attack bit off the ring. Cap still `0.086`. Riding the rail is not that swing. Live Defense fades with remaining RPM so dying Beys take a little more knock and RPM loss instead of chip-stalling. `impactMomentumState` follows the shove that landed on that Bey (not its own outgoing knock) but must not freeze orbit off — ease back into the ring (soft bowl) even while they are still sliding. Speed-target damping must not eat impact velocity while `impactMomentumState` is high. Separating overlaps still must not stack.
- Xtreme/Over need a knock into the opening (`lastImpactForce` at least `0.020`), not a parked clip. Smash credit lasts about 1.1s and needs force `0.028`. A smash that strong can still score after hit-stun fades. Light force only counts while they are still in the shove. Crossing a painted hole is the attempt: clash tangent is allowed (do not require a perfect radial line). Mouth-force hits after an X-Exit/X-Rail swing count as a clash, not rail carry. Classify as smash when force is `0.028` and heading is at least `0.28` off the rail (`0.12` in free play); otherwise rail or dump. Recovery decides climb vs score — do not silently bounce a real hole entry. Occupying a painted hole (center in, or a knocked disc overlapping the rim) is a made pocket: Over/Xtreme or recovery, not a sit. Light/parked clips can still bounce. A knock into Over/Xtreme at 80%+ RPM recovers about `0.45` (straight smash a bit less, glance a bit more). Smash is still the easiest KO. Generic rail pockets and healthy limp dumps climb more than a smash. `inCommittedFinishMouth` skips the solid bounce so a mouth-force knock can finish crossing (outward `0.0012`). Backside rail still does not re-hook. An X-Exit swing that then clashes gets a slight knock boost (`1.11`, still capped at `0.086`). Non-Attack bits on that swing use the Attack clash role so they hit with similar weight. Show RECOVERED over IMPACT/launch callouts when it happens. There is no 1.20s launch invuln bounce; X-Rail spawn sits on the rail, not in a hole. Do not add a timer to prevent self-KO.
- Live orbit follows Bit type, then stats. Home radius is a soft radial tendency, not a locked path. Free movement is a cartesian blend toward launch+RPM speed (`physicalSpeedTarget`) with a soft bowl toward home radius. Attack is slightly inside the rail at full RPM. By 50% RPM it sits just under the X-Rail/X-Exit so laps miss the ring. By the last 20% RPM Attack pulls into a tight ring like Non-Attack, with a little leftover aggression. Tired Attack bits must actually walk in — leftover rail-width speed is bled when they are outside home. Orbit must yield to knockback the same way Defense/Stamina do. Non-Attack Center launches start a bit wide (under the X-Rail, never a rail sling) then walk in quickly; Ball/Orb stay shorter and tighter than Point/Level. By 30% RPM every Non-Attack bit sits on the center pin so two tired tanks meet and clash instead of circling past each other. After a smash they can be moved off that ring toward Over/Xtreme without skating like a hockey puck, then settle. Do not polar-rewrite heading into a perfect circle. X-Exit and free hits on the closer bounce toward center with most leftover speed and a slightly stronger shove to middle. While `railEngaged`, ride speed is owned by the X-Rail engine; do not cruise-damp it. Cruise accelerates up to `physicalSpeedTarget` and only bleeds excess; skip it while impact/exit is hot. A smash or X-Exit/rail knock that is actually flying at Over/Xtreme can trigger a short slo-mo zoom (~50%, once per point, not for spin or center pokes). The green stadium outline stays fixed; an inner lens zooms toward the impact and is clipped so it never covers the HUD. Impact during that cam gets a small shake. Physics ticks stay `1/60`; only the clock feeding them slows. Stadium CSS size still must not scale knock.
- X-Exit leaves the rail toward left-center, center, or right-center with a small heading change. Do not force every exit down the exact middle. A free Bey that hits the X-Exit bounces toward stadium center with most of its speed and a slightly stronger shove to middle; it rides only if the existing hook already has a real CCW bite and momentum into the rail.
- Only the three painted holes (left Over, center Xtreme, right Over) can be left by a real outward smash. Wall, X-Rail mouth, and score share those SVG polygons. The rest of the lower X-Rail and wall stay solid. Light taps still should not score. A finish must actually cross into the hole, not graze the lower-rail rectangles that used to sit above the art.
- X-Rail capture uses remaining CCW + inward bite, not peak RPM. A real entry still has to hook from the inner face; the bite/speed bar is a little easier so near-misses ride instead of bouncing. Do not capture from the back of the rail or while already in a painted hole. Failed hooks bounce off the rail softer and keep a little CCW so a high-RPM Attack can still wind on instead of skating straight off. Low remaining RPM raises that hook bar so tired wide laps bounce instead of riding out the match. X-Exit closer still sends them toward middle with some orbit tangent. Ride speed is the speed they arrived with plus a light RPM drive. Exit speed is that carried rail speed. A rail-break ejector shoves toward stadium middle, not out through a pocket.
- Direct Clash aims at the opponent after both Beys spawn. Launch quality is aim accuracy (Perfect is tight, Horrible is wide). `impactMomentumState` stays high so they fly at each other instead of immediately orbiting.
- Center launches stay in the middle of the bowl but sit slightly toward that player's stadium side so two Center picks cannot spawn on top of each other. Attack-type bits still wind out toward their wide ring. Non-Attack bits wind into their own tighter orbit and must not sling out to the X-Rail like Attack. A small spin-correct tangent, not a random throw.
- X-Rail launches still start at the live lower corner via `SpinWarsXRailEngine.nearest`. Do not add a second invisible wall.
- CPU launch is locked at round start from the player's past techniques/tilts, never the live pick. Center-heavy habits get more X-Rail/Drop/Clash; X-Rail habits get rail contests and clashes, not Center; Clash habits get Center/rail/drop answers; Drop habits get Center and clash. CPU also avoids repeating itself.
- Lint/test/build: there is no project linter or test runner. Prove changes with a browser battle (Drop, Direct Clash, and X-Rail) plus any one-off `node` geometry asserts against `xrail-engine.js`.
